# Online Sales Analytics Dashboard – Excel

## Project Overview
Built an interactive, multi-panel sales analytics dashboard in Excel using Power Pivot, data modeling, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers to analyze customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends across multiple cities.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Conditional Formatting)
- **Power Pivot** for building the data model and handling large-scale relational data
- **Data Modeling** — established relationships across multiple tables (Customer, Order, Payment, Product) to enable cross-table analysis
- KPI cards for at-a-glance business metrics

## Dashboard Components
1. **Order Quantity by City** – Bar chart comparing order volume across 10+ cities, highlighting top-performing regions
2. **Customer Segmentation by Average Order Value** – Compares New, Regular, and VIP customer tiers, showing Regular customers as the highest-value segment
3. **Monthly Sales Trend (2024 vs 2025)** – Year-over-year line chart tracking seasonal performance and growth patterns
4. **Sales & Quantity by Category** – Dual pie charts breaking down revenue and units sold across product categories (Electronics, Accessories, Gaming, Home Office, Wearables, Stationery)
5. **Top 5 Products by Revenue** – Ranks best-selling SKUs (e.g., Office Chair, Smart Watch, Gaming Controller) by total revenue contribution
6. **Product Quantity Analysis** – Identifies top-moving products by unit volume

## Key Insights
- Regular customers generate the highest average order value (₹73.18), outperforming both New and VIP segments
- Electronics is the leading category by revenue share
- Sales showed a general upward trend in 2025 compared to 2024, with seasonal peaks
- A small set of hero products (Office Chair, Smart Watch, Gaming Controller) drive a disproportionate share of top-line revenue

## Key Business Questions I Answered

### Customer Behavior
- Which customer segment spends the most? (New vs. Regular vs. VIP)
- How often do customers repeat purchase?
- What's the average basket size and top order-driving cities?

### Product Analysis
- Which categories and products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most units (volume vs. value)?

### Trend Analysis
- How does sales performance compare year-over-year (2024 vs. 2025)?
- What's the overall business health at a glance? (KPIs)
